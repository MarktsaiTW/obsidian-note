## 📖 Journal
嘗試學習口說英文的工作流:
1. 先在Obsidian紀錄Playbook，格式為
	1. 觸發情境 / 原本想說的話 -> 想爭取時間思考
	2. 升級語塊（填空框架） -> _Let me unpack that real quick..._
	3. 我的真實造句（錄音練習用）-> "Let me unpack that real quick before we jump into the next step."
2. 將筆記丟到NotebookLM
3. 在開啟Gemini Live前，在NotebookLM撈出會話情境可能會用到的語塊，且一次不要超過8個
	**指令範本：**
	`我再來要將部分語塊交給Gemini Live練習口說，這次我想練習的主題為"___"，請從我的筆記挑出 6～8 個最適合口說會話用來形容事物的實用語塊，按外觀、體驗、優缺點、總評分類，每條附簡短中文與一格口語填空例句。`

4.  開啟Gemini Live新的Session，並給他要練習的會話情境，以及優先監督清單上的語塊
	指令範本:
	"Hey Gemini, we are going to practice spoken English via **Gemini Live**. Today's topic is "_xxx_".
	Here is my target phrase list from my notes:
	***Markdown***
	**Rules during our Live conversation:**
	1. Role-play as my conversation partner asking about a product or tool I recently used.
	2. **Monitor my vocabulary:** If I use simple or vague words where a phrase from my list fits better, gently jump in and remind me to use that specific phrase.
	3. Keep your spoken responses concise (1–2 sentences) so the conversation flows naturally.
	Reply with one short sentence to confirm, and I'll start Gemini Live."
